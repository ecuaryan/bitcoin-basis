# Redesign notes: entry lists as an aligned table on desktop (branch `redesign/entries`)

For the integrator: move the text below into `CHANGELOG.md` and `docs/design.md`, then delete this file.

## Proposed CHANGELOG entry

- **Entries read as a table on desktop.** Records' recent and all entries, and a wallet's Entries, line up in columns on a wide card: Date (month and day over the year), What (title and tags over a muted note), Wallet (not on a wallet's own page), Bitcoin (all 8 decimals, signed in or out, a move's network fee under it) and Dollars (with what they were: paid, received, worth then; "—" when there are none). On a phone the rows stay compact and now show the full 8-decimal Bitcoin and say what the dollars were ("$680.00 paid", "$920.00 worth then").
- **A move on a wallet's page shows what left that wallet.** "Moved to Cold card" shows the coins plus the network fee (−0.0500 1840), with "0.0500 0000 arrived · fee 0.0000 1840" under the title, so the rows add up to the wallet's balance; on the wallet the coins reached it reads "Moved from Strike" (+ what arrived). A payout out of a frozen wallet shows unsigned on that wallet's page ("Paid out to …"), since it does not change that wallet's coins.
- Retirement purchases in entry lists read "Bought" with the Retirement tag.

## Proposed docs/design.md paragraph (replaces the "Entry lists" bullet)

- **Entry lists** (`EntryTable`, `screens/entry-table.tsx`; words and figures from `rowLine` in `lib/entry-row.ts`, on top of `describeEntry`): every list of entries (Records' recent and all entries, a wallet's entries) comes from `entryRows` in `lib/entry-list.ts` and renders `EntryTable`; tapping a row opens `EntrySheet` (details, history, `Edit purchase` / `Remove this purchase`). The box is a container. Under 34rem of card (every phone) a row is the glyph, the title with its tags over `wallet · note · date` (with the year), and the signed 8-decimal BTC over its dollars and what they were (`$680.00 paid`; a move's `fee 0.0000 1840`; a loan record's dollars over `paid`). From 34rem it is one grid, each row a subgrid: Date (month and day over the year) · What (glyph; title, tags and the muted note: income or spending description, loss cause, import origin, a loan record's loan) · Wallet (`Strike → Cold card` for a move; `—` for loan, settings and year records; dropped on a wallet's own page) · Bitcoin (`Amount` without `short`, `alwaysBtc`, signed `+`/`−`, an unsigned figure keeps an invisible `+` so digits align; a move's fee under it) · Dollars (`$680.00` over `paid`, `received` or `worth then`; `—` when none). On a wallet's own page the figures are that wallet's: a move out is `Moved to …` with what left (`sats + feeSats`, signed −) and `… arrived · fee …` as its note, a move in is `Moved from …` (+ what arrived), so the signed rows add up to the wallet's balance (a unit test checks the demo book). Column headings are visual only (`aria-hidden`); DOM order is the reading order (title first) and each cell carries screen-reader commas and words (`BTC`, `fee`, `no Bitcoin`, `no dollars`), so a row reads "Bought, Strike · Nov 18, 2026, +0.0074 2665 BTC, $680.00 paid". Settings changes are hidden unless filtered for, balance confirmations are never listed (they show as `Confirmed Nov 21` on the wallet), and every count is the number of rows shown. Strikethrough means removed and always comes with a `Removed` badge (its amounts muted); `Edited` appears only for the person's own edits; tags: `Needs a decision`, `Removed`, `Edited`, the loan badge, `Retirement`.

## Notes and things left alone

- The 34rem threshold is one value for both variants: every phone card is under it (the 560px column leaves about 30rem), the Records list on desktop is over 43rem even at 1024px, and a wallet's Entries card (three fifths of the page beside the balance) is over it from about 1300px wide; below that it keeps the compact rows.
- On a phone a wallet's own rows still name the wallet (`Strike · Nov 20, 2026`), as before; only the wide table drops the column.
- `centsLabel` in `describeEntry` still says `worth` (the Log review uses it as a field label, "Worth"); only list rows say `worth then`.
- Not changed: the `EntrySheet` title for a retirement purchase stays "Bought (retirement)".
- Unrelated, found while testing: with Hide amounts on, axe reports `label-content-name-mismatch` on the masked interactive `Amount` button (`.text-inherit`, e.g. the wallet page's big balance): its name "Amount hidden. Hold to show." does not contain the visible dots. Existing behaviour, not touched here; the new e2e runs axe before turning Hide amounts on.
- Phone entry titles no longer clamp to two lines (`line-clamp-2` removed): with the full 8-decimal BTC beside them, a long gift title needed a third line and `phone-parity.spec.ts` requires titles never be cut off.
