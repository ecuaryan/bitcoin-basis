# Redesign notes: What if → Sell plan cards (branch redesign/sell)

For the integrator: move the text below into CHANGELOG.md and docs/design.md, then delete this file.

## Proposed CHANGELOG entry

- **Sell: plan cards you can read straight across.** Every plan card (Oldest first, Lowest tax, Lots you picked) shows the same rows in the same places, lined up across cards: the tax as the big number ("Tax this adds", or "Tax this saves" in green) with "Federal $X + state $Y" under it, what you keep after tax with its math ("$23,703.10 sale − $0.00 tax"), short-term, long-term, losses left for next year, the share on Estimated or Unknown cost, and the lots used. Empty figures ($0.00, 0%) are muted. The plan the planner prefers gets a small pill ("Least tax", "Saves the most", or "Keeps more losses" when the tax is the same). Under the cards, "Lots used in …" lists the chosen plan's lots (bought, term, Bitcoin, cost, sold for, gain or loss) with a Total row that matches the card's short-term + long-term. A third plan (after picking lots) sits full width under the two on a phone. Federal and state tax now always add up to the total to the cent (the engine splits the total by largest remainder).

## Proposed docs/design.md paragraph (Components list)

- **Sell plan cards** (`PlanCard`, `PlanChoice`, `LotsUsedTable` in `screens/whatif/sell-parts.tsx`; logic in `sell-logic.ts`): the plans sit in one CSS grid (`PlanChoice`, a radio group named "Which lots to sell", under a "Which lots to sell" header with the lot "?"); each card is a `button role="radio"` spanning 8 rows as a subgrid (`row-span-8 grid-rows-subgrid`), so every row lines up across cards. Rows, in order: the title (with a radio dot); "Tax this adds"/"Tax this saves" (`taxShown`, green when it saves) with "Federal $X + state $Y" (the engine's `federalTaxCents` + `stateTaxCents`, which add up to `totalTaxCents`); "You keep after tax" (`afterTaxCents`) with "$X sale − $Y tax" (or "+ $Y tax saved"); Short-term and Long-term gain or loss (`termLabel`, green/red, $0.00 muted); Losses left for next year (amber on the one plan that leaves the most, `mostCarried`; $0.00 muted); On Estimated or Unknown cost (`riskPercentFromBp`, 0% muted, masked by Hide amounts); Lots used (date range and count). Figures are `PlainMoney` (no button inside the card's button). The pill (`bestPills`) follows the engine's `isBetterPlan` (less tax, then more loss left for next year): every plan no other beats gets it, none when all tie; "Least tax", "Saves the most" (negative tax) or "Keeps more losses" (won on losses left at equal tax); it is the card's `aria-describedby`. Two columns on a phone (a third plan full width underneath), three on desktop. The selected card keeps the orange border and gradient. Under the cards, `LotsUsedTable` ("Lots used in Lowest tax · 17 lots · Feb–Nov 2026", a disclosure, open to start for 8 lots or fewer): one grid, rows as subgrids, in a container: under 40rem Bought (term and tier under it) · Bitcoin (cost under it) · Gain or loss ("sold $X" under it); from 40rem Bought · Term · Bitcoin · Cost · Sold for · Gain or loss. Rows are the engine's `SalePlan.pieces` (proceeds split by sats with `splitProportional`; a dual-basis gift shows the basis the rule used, so sold − cost = gain); the Total row is the plan's own `costCents`, `proceedsCents` and `gainCents` (= short-term + long-term). Headings are visual only; cells carry screen-reader words.

## Engine changes (in this branch, spec updated in docs/engine-spec.md)

- `SaleEvaluation.pieces` (each lot's part: sats, cost, proceeds, gain, term, tier; adds up to the plan) and `SaleEvaluation.afterTaxCents` (proceeds − total tax).
- `isBetterPlan` exported (the planner's existing "better" rule, unchanged).
- `capitalTaxEffect` and `incrementalTax`: federal and state are now split from the rounded total by largest remainder (`addingParts`), so federal + state = total exactly. Before, each was rounded on its own and could miss the total by a cent (for example 3 cents of short-term gain: federal 0.36¢, state 0.159¢, total 0.519¢ gave 0 + 0 ≠ 1). Totals are unchanged; a part can move by one cent. The existing engine test that pinned the old behavior was updated.

## Unrelated problems noticed (not fixed here)

- `taxForYear` still rounds federal and state separately (its total can differ from federal + state by a cent; a property test allows it). Tax season shows only totals today, so nothing visible is wrong, but if a federal/state split is ever shown there it should use `addingParts` too.
- The old plan cards put `<Money>` inside the card's radio button; under Hide amounts that made a `role="button"` span inside a button (nested interactive). The new cards use `PlainMoney`. Other places may still nest `Money` in buttons.
